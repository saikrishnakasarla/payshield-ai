# PayShield AI — Hackathon Pitch & Demo Script (Under 3 Minutes)

**Target Duration**: 2 minutes 45 seconds  
**Roles**: Presenter (Merchant Persona) & Technical Lead

---

### [0:00 – 0:30] Hook & Problem Statement
> *"Hello judges! For small businesses, unexpected chargebacks and payment fraud are devastating. According to industry data, exceeding just a 1% chargeback rate puts a merchant's payment processing in jeopardy. But small merchants don't have $50,000 fraud teams or data scientists to decipher cryptic risk codes.*  
> 
> *Meet **PayShield AI** — the explainable financial protection assistant for PayPal merchants. PayShield AI pairs a transparent, deterministic risk engine with Google Gemini intelligence to monitor transactions, explain why an alert fired in plain English, and empower merchants to make confident fulfillment decisions."*

---

### [0:30 – 1:15] Overview Dashboard & Pattern Telemetry
> *(Navigate to Overview Dashboard)*  
> *"Here is the PayShield command center. Notice our dark cybersecurity design built for fast cognitive processing. Right away, merchants see their Monitored Processing Volume, Flagged Transactions, and our 99.4% Deterministic Accuracy.*  
> 
> *Our dynamic Detection Timeline tracks high versus medium risk spikes over 24-hour, 7-day, and 30-day windows. We clearly display that these are synthetic demo transactions and explainable recommendations. Down below, we categorize fraud by pattern — such as velocity spikes and baseline mismatches — alongside high-risk geographic origin telemetry."*

---

### [1:15 – 2:00] Explainable AI Dossier & Merchant Investigation
> *(Click on high-risk transaction TXN-847291 to open the Investigation Dossier)*  
> *"Let's investigate this flagged order: Alex Smith for $2,847.00. Notice our circular Risk Gauge shows an 82/100 High Risk rating. But instead of an opaque black box, PayShield AI provides full mathematical transparency:*
> 1. *First, the deterministic engine reveals the exact heuristic rules triggered — a 35x baseline jump, a foreign IP mismatch, and a velocity burst.*
> 2. *Second, Google Gemini delivers an explainable synthesis in plain English, highlighting the evidence while honestly declaring what is uncertain.*
> 3. *Third, it generates an actionable merchant verification checklist.*  
> 
> *Because we respect customer trust, our actions are review-only. We can add an audit note and mark it 'Escalate for Human Review', creating a permanent compliance record."*

---

### [2:00 – 2:40] Live PayPal Sandbox Checkout & Server-Side Intercept
> *(Navigate to PayPal Sandbox Interceptor)*  
> *"Now let's demonstrate our PayPal integration. PayShield connects directly to the PayPal Orders API in Sandbox mode. Let's create a test order for a $125 Cloud Suite license.*  
> 
> *The amount is strictly validated on our FastAPI server. Once the buyer completes checkout via the PayPal Smart Button, our backend captures the payment, verifies the COMPLETED status directly with PayPal, and instantly intercepts the transaction with PayShield's risk engine.*  
> 
> *Look at that: verified capture reference, clean receipt, and an instant low-risk score (12/100) with zero customer friction!"*

---

### [2:40 – 3:00] Closing & Impact
> *(Navigate to Insights & Reports)*  
> *"Finally, our Insights engine benchmarks the merchant's Chargeback Defense Score and allows one-click export of the entire audit ledger to CSV.*  
> 
> *PayShield AI turns payment security from an intimidating threat into an intuitive, explainable superpower for every PayPal merchant. Thank you!"*
